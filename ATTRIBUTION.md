# Attribution & asset licenses

Every non-code asset in this repo, where it came from, and its license.
Everything tracked here is freely licensed (CC0, MIT or Apache-2.0).

**Custom scenes are not part of the repo.** Rooms you add yourself live in
the gitignored `scenes/` folder on your own machine or server (see
[scenes/README.md](scenes/README.md)). Credit each one in its `scene.json`
(`credit`, `license`, `source`) and follow its license yourself. That
matters most when you put a build that includes them on a public host
(`LOCAL_SCENES=off npm run build` leaves them out).

## Pool room (built-in default room): Poly Haven, CC0 1.0

The room itself (walls, sliding glass door, desk, radio) is built from boxes
in code (`src/room/procedural-room.ts`, `desk.ts`). Its only two image files
come from Poly Haven, released under **CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain)**:
<https://polyhaven.com/license>. Attribution is not required, but we credit
the authors anyway.
